Delete your Driftfeed data

Driftfeed (com.hartmannstudios.driftfeed) by Hartmann Studios. Driftfeed has no accounts.

Data on your phone

Your profile, settings, feed and posts are stored only on your phone. To delete them: Android Settings > Apps > Driftfeed > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling also resets the Firebase app instance identifier.

Reports and bug reports you sent

To delete a content report or bug report you sent from the app (including any name, email or screenshot you added), email me@charleshartmann.com with the subject "Driftfeed data deletion" and tell us roughly when you sent it and what it said. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. Nothing is kept after deletion.

Analytics, crash reports and ads

Analytics and crash data are not linked to your name or email. Firebase Analytics data expires automatically (14 months at most) and crash reports after 90 days. To request deletion sooner, email us the same way. To reset or delete the advertising ID used by Google ads, go to Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
